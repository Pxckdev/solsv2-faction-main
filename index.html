Tiers, requirements, upkeep, and where to apply for your faction.

Faction Tier Information

Every faction starts at Tier 1 and works its way up through consistent, quality roleplay. Weapon access matches the tiers on the Faction Helper.

TIER 1

Street level

New and approved factions. Prove you can keep up quality RP.

  • Weapon Limits: Capped At 6 Tier 1 Weapons & 2 Higher Tier Weapons.
  • Member Limit: 15
  • Turf: One Block, Allowed to Expand Play Area With Higher Tiers.
  • How to get it: approved faction application
TIER 1.5

Established

Factions that have shown steady activity and clean RP.

  • Weapon Limites: Capped At 8 Tier 1.5 Weapons & 2 Higher Tier Weapons.
  • Member Limit: 25.
  • Turf: Play Area Maxed At 3 Blocks.
  • How to get it: Shows Serious Roleplay & Follows Upkeep.
TIER 2

Top tier

The most active, respected factions in the city.

  • Weapon Limits: Capped At 10 Tier 2 Weapons & 2 Lower Tier Weapons.
  • Member Limit: 30
  • Turf: A larger Selection Of Areas / Blocks To Play.
  • How to get it: 4 weeks at Tier 1.5, all upkeep met and no active strikes

Tier changes are decided by Faction Management. Meeting the minimum doesn't guarantee a tier up.

Faction Requirements

What your faction needs before you apply.

  • Keep at least 8 Members In your faction discord.
  • All Members must read roe & server guidelines .
  • Must have a clear and simple rank system logged, NormallyIt Goes Resident Official Member Trial Council, Council, Leadership.
  • Follow the Faction ROE in every interaction.

Faction Upkeep Requirements

What every faction has to keep doing each week to keep its tier.

  • Atleast5 Faction rp showcases & 10 Screens Every 7 Days.
  • A Minimum Of 35 Interactions Every 14 Days. .
  • Showing Serious Faction Roleplay In All Non Attack And Defense Logs logged, Including All Attack And Defense Logs..
  • All Attack And Defense Logs Must Be Clipped With Either Nivida Or AMD And Also Must Be 3 Minutes Long If Requested From IFM Or Opposing Faction.

If upkeep isn't met

  1. 1 week missed: warning from Faction Management.
  2. 2 weeks missed: Another Warning Maybe A Faction Strike.
  3. 3 strikes: the faction is disbanded.

Faction application

Fill in every question as best you can. You'll get a Discord DM when Faction Management reviews it.

  • You can only have one application pending at a time. Once it's accepted or denied, you can apply again.
  • Use the toolbar on the big boxes for fonts, text sizes, links, photos, GIFs and videos. Press 👁 Preview to see how it'll look.
  • Make sure your Discord username is exactly right, or we can't message you.
